02 / ABOUT

Behind the worlds.

A little about me.

I'm a multidisciplinary game designer and visual artist working across visual storytelling, narrative design and gameplay systems. My approach combines artistic intuition with analytical thinking, exploring how characters, environments, mechanics and narrative can work together to shape the way players perceive and experience a world.

I'm particularly drawn to psychological horror as a way of exploring internal experiences such as uncertainty, isolation, distorted perception and loss of control. Rather than simply telling the player what a character is experiencing, I'm interested in using mechanics, narrative and visual design to let the player inhabit a version of that experience themselves.

Immersion sits at the centre of my design process. I'm particularly interested in player agency, environmental storytelling and the point where something familiar becomes subtly wrong. I want to create experiences that stay with the player after they stop playing, whether through discomfort, recognition, curiosity or the need for a moment to process what just happened.
