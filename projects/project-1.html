Wakey Wakey

A psychological horror game exploring insomnia, dreams and unreliable perception. Wake up, examine your surroundings and determine whether you have truly escaped the dream.

01 / OVERVIEW

Concept

Wakey Wakey is a psychological horror game exploring insomnia, dreams and unreliable perception. The player's goal is to reach Day 21 while repeatedly waking and determining whether they have actually returned to reality.

As the days progress, waking becomes more difficult and familiar surroundings become less reliable. The aim is to make the player experience the uncertainty rather than simply observe it.

02 / MY ROLE

Game Design & Creative Direction

Wakey Wakey is a collaborative two-person project. My role includes:

  • Core concept and gameplay loop
  • Gameplay mechanics and player experience
  • Narrative design
  • Visual direction
  • Environment and object design
  • 2D asset creation and animation

Programming and technical implementation are primarily handled by the other developer, with design and feasibility discussed collaboratively.

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Making the player question what is real.

The bedroom as a baseline

The player repeatedly returns to the same bedroom, allowing them to learn what "normal" looks like. As the space becomes familiar, even small inconsistencies can become noticeable and unsettling.

The 21-day loop

The player's goal is simple: reach Day 21. Repeated cycles of sleeping, waking and questioning reality are intentionally frustrating, drawing from experiences of false awakenings, lucid dreaming and sleep paralysis.

The long-term objective remains clear while the player's immediate reality becomes increasingly unreliable.

Designing the wake-up mechanic

I am exploring whether players should actively search for inconsistencies, or whether abnormalities should gradually reveal themselves before the challenge shifts towards waking up.

The final approach will be determined by which creates the strongest uncertainty while keeping the player engaged with their surroundings.

04 / PROCESS & ITERATION

Developing the experience.

The core loop is established, while mechanics, pacing and visual design continue to be tested and refined. Current development focuses on finding the balance between observation, uncertainty and the player's struggle to wake up.

Visual development

The bedroom environment is currently being developed and refined alongside the game's objects, animations and visual distortions.
